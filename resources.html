01 · Getting started

What OgaProp is designed to do

OgaProp is being designed as a verified property marketplace. The product combines property discovery with visible verification information so users can see what checks support a listing instead of relying on a generic trust badge.

02 · Marketplace

Finding and comparing property

The marketplace is the central property browsing experience. Users can filter by sale or rent, neighbourhood, property type, bedrooms and price, then open a property to review its details and verification status.

Open marketplace
03 · Verification

What “OgaProp Verified” should mean

Verification is intended to be a record, not a yes-or-no flag. A listing can show the checks completed for property existence, participant identity, title documents, land records, litigation searches and physical inspection where applicable.

Property existence

Physical property record and location confirmation.

Document review

Relevant title and supporting documents reviewed.

Identity & authority

Participant identity and right to list considered.

Transaction readiness

Additional checks can support later transaction steps.

04 · Inspections

From listing to physical confirmation

Eligible listings can support physical inspection requests. For remote users, the same journey can support guided video inspections and evidence recorded against the property.

05 · Diaspora

A property journey designed for distance

The diaspora experience is planned around remote property search, verification visibility, guided video inspections and dedicated support. The goal is to reduce dependence on informal third parties when the buyer cannot be physically present.

06 · Transactions

The planned transaction journey

The longer-term flow is discovery → verification → enquiry → inspection → offer → payment or financing → completion. For the presentation MVP, the website demonstrates the discovery, verification, enquiry and inspection parts of that journey.
