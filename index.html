Questions people should ask before trusting a listing.

What does OgaProp Verified mean?

It means the listing has passed the verification checks shown on its verification record, including the relevant property and documentation checks completed by OgaProp.

Can I request a physical inspection?

Yes. Inspection requests are part of the planned property journey and will be available from eligible listings.

Is OgaProp built for Nigerians abroad?

Yes. The diaspora experience is a core part of OgaProp, including remote inspection support, property verification and guided transaction support.
